Hi, I'm Jaroslav

I’m a product manager, and the question that keeps me writing is, “How do you turn messy notes into work you can actually ship?”

To answer that, I use this site as a small public notebook: essays, FAQs, and a few pages I keep coming back to when a project gets noisy.

Over the last few years, I’ve helped teams decide what to build, what to cut, and what to write down before the meeting starts.

The point is not a perfect system. It is a trusted place outside your head where the important ideas wait until you need them — then you can turn them into a spec, a post, or a decision.

The goal is simpler weeks: fewer tabs, less rereading, and more finished work with a little more ease.
