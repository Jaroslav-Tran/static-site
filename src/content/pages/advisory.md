## The problem you probably have

Something in your product isn't converting the way it should, and you're not sure why. Maybe self-serve isn't pulling its weight. Maybe you're staring at an integration roadmap with no real way to rank it. Maybe you just need someone who's actually done this before to look at the whole thing and tell you where it's actually broken.

That's the work. I spent 8+ years as a B2B SaaS PM doing exactly this — most recently a year at CloudTalk as Senior Integrations PM, before that at Productboard across Growth and Integrations. Now I do it independently, for founders who need the same thinking without hiring a full-time PM.

---

## What I actually help with

**Does PLG even make sense for us?**
Not every product should be self-serve. I'll tell you honestly if yours shouldn't, instead of selling you a funnel you don't need.

**Why isn't our self-service funnel converting?**
The real question is where it's actually breaking. Sometimes it's the funnel. Just as often it's onboarding, a design choice quietly confusing people, or the fact that nobody's ever clearly said why you're better than the spreadsheet they're already using. I go check rather than assume.

**What should our integration strategy even be?**
Which ones to build or improve, in what order, and why. This is the area I've gone deepest on, though I treat it as one lever, not the whole answer.

**How do we prioritize any of this?**
I weigh market signal, technical risk, and team reality against each other, so the loudest voice in the room doesn't win by default.

## How we work together

**One-off calls.** You've got one specific, annoying problem. We spend an hour or two taking it apart together — no deck, no retainer, just working through it.

**PLG / product audits.** I go through your funnel, onboarding, and product experience the way I'd go through my own team's — talk to a handful of your users if needed, dig through your data, and hand you back a prioritized list of what's actually broken and why, not a generic checklist.

**Ongoing advisory.** For teams who want someone close enough to be useful on a recurring basis — reviewing decisions, sanity-checking a roadmap, being a second brain on product calls — without becoming another line item on payroll.

There's no fixed package here. What actually makes sense depends on where you are and what's broken — that's exactly what the first conversation is for.

## How an engagement usually goes

**1. A short intro conversation.** No pitch, no slides. You tell me what's going on, I ask questions until I actually understand the problem, and we figure out together whether this is a one-off call, an audit, or something ongoing.

**2. I go look, not guess.** If it's an audit, this is where I dig into your data, your product, and — when useful — your users directly. I don't diagnose from a framework; I diagnose from what's actually there.

**3. A prioritized, specific answer.** Not a 40-slide deck. A clear, ranked list of what's actually wrong, why it's wrong, and what to do about it first — the same way I'd brief my own team.

**4. Optional follow-through.** Some people take the list and run with it themselves. Others want me around while they implement it, or want a recurring check-in. Both are fine — I'd rather match the engagement to what you need than upsell you into more than that.
