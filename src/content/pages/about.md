This is a small static website. Pages and blog posts are written in Markdown, then turned into HTML with a short Node script.

The goal is to keep publishing easy: open a `.md` file, write, and rebuild.

## What you can do here

- Publish a landing page, about page, FAQ, and contact page
- Add blog posts as Markdown files
- Collect emails with a ConvertKit form
- Receive messages with a simple contact form

Change the site name and description in `src/site.json`. Change the look in `src/styles/style.css`.
